Is it reading a book one quiet afternoon in the shade of a hundred year tree? Is it watching, in silence, as birds hop from branch to branch, heedless? Is it a kind word from a stranger? Is it the unexpected generosity of another soul wandering less lost? Is it a butterfly unmindful of time? Is it the minute of silence to observe yourself? Is it knowing that which is worth your attention? Is it sitting in your favorite spot sipping tea while writing? Or the money to afford a million such teas? Or the wildflower that needs no human to tend it? The quest of ancients and modernists. Yet, it is also every human’s quest. Define it. Feel it. Cherish it. Untouched by an unkind world. To read is to discover another’s discovery of it. To write is to leave a trail — in case someone understands your quest too.
